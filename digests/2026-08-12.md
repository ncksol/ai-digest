## OpenAI chief operating officer announces his departure

Brad Lightcap published the note he sent to the OpenAI team saying he is leaving the company to start something new. He wrote that he joined in 2018 and built the first versions of most of the company's operations and business functions, including finance, legal, people, corporate security, go-to-market and partnerships. He said he will remain for the next few weeks and that he has been focused on what would stand in the way of mission success, with more to share soon.
