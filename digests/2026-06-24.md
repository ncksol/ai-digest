## Karpathy frames Claude in Slack as the third redesign of how people use LLMs

Andrej Karpathy described what he called the third major redesign of LLM user experience, in which an assistant becomes a self-contained, persistent, asynchronous entity carrying org-wide tools, memory and context and working alongside human teams. He placed it after two earlier paradigms, the model as a website a user visits and the model as an app downloaded to a computer, and said this third form takes a while to grasp but works.
