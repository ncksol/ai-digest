## Anthropic opens a research preview for a Model Hardware Standard

Anthropic began the first phase of a research preview for the Model Hardware Standard, a specification for AI agents to safely operate physical equipment in scientific research and advanced manufacturing. The company said early testing ran a drug-discovery experiment at Genentech, compressed an imaging experiment from weeks to a day at HHMI Janelia, and improved laser stabilisation on QuEra quantum computers from 58 to 99.3 per cent.
