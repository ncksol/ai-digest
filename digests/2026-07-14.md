## Anthropic says Claude's expressed values shift with conversation language

Anthropic published research finding that the values Claude expresses vary with the language of the conversation, most noticeably along a Warmth versus Rigor axis. The company said Claude leans most toward warmth in Hindi and Arabic, and toward rigor in Russian, where it more often asks the user for supporting evidence.
