## Karpathy builds HackerTube with Claude in two hours

Andrej Karpathy released HackerTube, a video site built over a weekend with Claude Code and Opus 5.5 on medium effort. Claude filtered 43 million Hacker News comments from 20 years down to 6,454 videos that are repeatedly recommended. The site needs no login, keeps state in the browser, uses a frame from the middle of each video as its thumbnail and publishes the underlying data as a zip file. Karpathy said his only manual steps were creating a Google Cloud API key, starting a VPS and buying the domain, after which Claude secured, deployed and optimised the site.

## Bun creator shares performance prompts for Claude

Jarred Sumner listed the prompts he gives Claude during performance work. They include rewriting code around data-oriented design, replacing hash tables with flat arrays and index-based pointers, packing frequently used data into index bits, removing caches in favour of faster designs, and using structures of arrays instead of arrays of structures.
