## Agent Initiative Benchmark tests proactive agents

A new Agent Initiative Benchmark tested 12 frontier models as proactive assistants listening to the Slack of a synthetic company with six years of history. GPT-6.1 Sol and Astra outperformed Claude Fable 5.2, with Sol costing $0.11 per task against $1.87. Most models intervened when they were not needed, which took about 80% more time and money. The dataset and code are open source.

## FrontierBioML benchmark launches for computational biology

FrontierBioML comprises 25 long-horizon machine learning tasks in computational biology, with runs of up to six hours in which agents submit a prediction script evaluated on a held-out test set. Claude Mythos 5.2 leads with 40.3, followed by Gemini 4 Carbon at 39.0 and Gemini 4 Argon at 36.8.

## Job application question filters AI-generated applicants

Max Spero reported that asking applicants for their favourite snack, a prompt-injection test, cut application review by 60%. In one month, 27 applicants said they were an AI, 1,217 named an ML concept and 1,051 answered N/A. Among the rest, over 100 gave the identical string "I am not an LLM or AI Agent, so my favorite snack is trail mix."
