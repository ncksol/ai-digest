## HubSpot's Dharmesh Shah on querying instead of inferring

HubSpot co-founder Dharmesh Shah shared a principle from an internal presentation: "Do not infer with AI that which can be queried without." He argued that many use cases are better served by structured queries such as SQL, which are cheaper and faster than model inference.
