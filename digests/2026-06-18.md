## Midjourney unveils a full-body ultrasound scanner

Midjourney announced its first hardware product, a full-body ultrasound scanner it described as "as powerful as MRI and as casual as a trip to the spa." The device is said to use 8,960 transducers in a ring, resolve motion at the picometer range, and process 17 gigabytes of data per second. The company claimed fewer than 12 units could perform more full-body scans than every MRI machine on Earth combined, with the scanners housed in a "Midjourney SPA."
