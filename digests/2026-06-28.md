## DeepSeek releases DSpark speculative decoding and open-sources its training framework

DeepSeek published DSpark, a speculative decoding method that the company reported boosts inference throughput by between 51 percent and 400 percent on its V4 Flash and Pro models. DeepSeek also open-sourced DeepSpec, the training framework behind the technique, and indicated the method works with other models including Gemma and Qwen.

## Cambridge and NVIDIA publish a paper revisiting the Gödel Machine

A post described a new paper from Cambridge and NVIDIA that revisits the Gödel Machine, a concept proposed by a German scientist in 2003 for a system that could prove its own improvements and rewrite itself. The original idea was described as mathematically rigorous but impractical, and the paper situates it against two decades of subsequent work on self-improving systems.
