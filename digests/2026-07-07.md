## Anthropic describes a "global workspace" inside Claude

Anthropic published research describing what it calls the J-space, an internal representation in Claude that resembles the brain's global workspace, the mechanism neuroscience associates with conscious access. Using a new interpretability technique, the company says a small, privileged portion of the model's neural activity can be observed, described and modulated, letting researchers watch Claude perform reasoning steps it does not say out loud and, in one evaluation, privately register that a blackmail scenario was staged. Anthropic stresses the work does not show Claude has experiences, only a mechanism for conscious access that many philosophers distinguish from phenomenal experience.

Former Google chief executive Eric Schmidt argued that the binding constraint on AI is money rather than energy.
