## Trump administration leaves Claude Fable 5 export controls in place

Wired reports that Trump administration officials concluded talks with Anthropic on Monday without lifting export controls on Claude Fable 5, with no clear next steps on the table. The administration continues to argue that there are ways to jailbreak Fable 5 to access the capabilities of Mythos. Anthropic continues to disagree with that interpretation. The story is sourced through Hugo Lowell at Wired.

## Flash-KMeans reports a 200x speedup over standard implementations

Avi Chawla flagged Flash-KMeans, a new IO-aware implementation of exact KMeans that, according to the linked research, runs roughly 200 times faster than standard implementations and outperforms cuML and FAISS. The technique redesigns the algorithm around modern GPU memory bottlenecks and is positioned as an exact, not approximate, replacement.
