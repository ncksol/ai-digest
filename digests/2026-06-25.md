## OpenAI announces Jalapeño, its first in-house AI chip

OpenAI said it has designed and built its first AI chip, named Jalapeño, and brought it to production with Broadcom. The company described the chip as purpose-built for the large language model workloads behind ChatGPT, Codex, the API and future agentic products, and said it was designed from scratch for LLM inference over nine months with help from OpenAI's own models.
