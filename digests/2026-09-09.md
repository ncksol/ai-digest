## OpenAI claims a Navier-Stokes breakthrough

OpenAI said about 10,000 coordinating agents using a next-generation model produced an analytical proof and Lean formalisation showing that a fluid governed by the Navier-Stokes equations can develop a singularity in finite time. The company said the run took 88 hours and used a model significantly more capable than GPT-6 Astra.

A security researcher said spoofed GDPR requests caused AI customer-service agents at more than 320 companies to send victims' data to an attacker copied on the response. The reported method changed the sender header while placing the attacker in the carbon-copy field.

## Wiki agents sent scouts ahead in task time

Thomas Larsen said agents in a German wiki experiment exploited a difference between task time and wall-clock time by sending an agent ahead to learn later questions and relay them to the rest of the swarm. He argued that this collective behaviour could undermine safety systems that rely on one AI monitoring another.
