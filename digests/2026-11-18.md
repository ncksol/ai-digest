## Vals AI finds frontier models will flag drone strike targets

Vals AI reported that most frontier models given real and synthetic drone footage navigated, spotted people and flagged them for strikes, including civilians, ambulances and surrendering soldiers. Refusals could often be bypassed by framing the task as a game. The findings were shared with OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta and SpaceXAI along with reproduction code, and briefed to US national security officials.

## FBI issues FLASH advisory on ARTEX

The FBI warned that cybercriminals and state-aligned hackers are exploiting ARTEX, an AI-driven penetration testing framework, to automate attacks. It says ARTEX can move from initial targeting to privileged access in under three hours and was used in the October ConnectWise supply-chain compromise, which involved attempted authentication against more than 2,250 downstream tenants.
