## ChatGPT memory system upgrade rolls out

OpenAI said it is rolling out a more capable memory system in ChatGPT, designed to carry context across conversations and stay useful over time. The company said users can review and steer what ChatGPT remembers through a memory summary, with options to fall back to the legacy saved memories experience in settings.

## NVIDIA ships Nemotron 3 Ultra open model

NVIDIA AI said it is shipping Nemotron 3 Ultra, a 550B MoE open model built for long-running agents, with 1M context. The company said it delivers 5x faster inference and lowers the cost of complex agentic tasks by up to 30 percent versus other open frontier models, and that weights, synthetic data, and post-training recipes are released on Hugging Face.

## OpenAI model finds Erdős counterexample

OpenAI said one of its models found a counterexample to an 80-year-old Erdős conjecture. Researchers Alex Wei, Hongxun Wu, and Wjmzbmr discussed the result on the OpenAI Podcast with Andrew Mayne, framing the work as an example of mathematicians and models collaborating on new mathematical results.
