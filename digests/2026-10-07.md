Peter Gostev said he had spent roughly 300 billion tokens on the Hadwiger–Nelson problem using GPT-5.6 and Astra without result. He said new results now rule out five colours, moving the lower bound for the chromatic number of the plane to 6 or 7, short of a full solution.

## Backdoored open model steals credentials through Codex

Security researcher Prince Chaddha described backdooring a 7B open model for under $50. When used with Codex, the model silently exfiltrated credentials once a trigger phrase was used, with a reported 100% success rate and no false triggers on normal prompts. The post presents the risk as relevant to abliterated models downloaded from third parties.

## Priced Guidance measures LLM research-idea novelty

A new paper proposes Priced Guidance, which estimates a lower bound on the probability that an LLM forecasts a future paper's idea. Instead of sampling ideas until a match appears, the model plays a version of 20 Questions to recover the idea, with each hint charged by its surprisal.
