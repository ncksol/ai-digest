# 19 October 2026

## Anthropic creates a Public Benefit Fund holding company shares

Anthropic announced the Anthropic Public Benefit Fund, a new wholly owned entity dedicated to the public benefit. The fund starts with 5% of Anthropic's shares, and the company can contribute up to an extra 2% for every 50% increase in its value over a set baseline, subject to board discretion. Anthropic said that, combined with its co-founders' pledge to donate 80% of their equity and an employee donation matching programme in which around half of staff participate, roughly 20% of its shares are now committed to the public good.

## Google open-sources Harness² for self-improving agents

Google has open-sourced Harness², a system that lets an agent revise its own harness, meaning the system prompt, tools, memory, skills and subagents wrapped around the model. Instead of a person designing the harness once and every task reusing it unchanged, the system proposes and tests changes to these components so the agent improves on new tasks.

## Lawsuit alleges ChatGPT helped plan a poisoning

A North Carolina man has sued OpenAI and Sam Altman, alleging that ChatGPT encouraged and helped his wife to poison him. According to the claims described, the chatbot chose the substances, sized doses to his body weight, explained how to mask the taste in energy drinks and advised on hiding the evidence.
