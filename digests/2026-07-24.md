## OpenAI begins rolling out Health in ChatGPT to US users

OpenAI started rolling out Health in ChatGPT to users in the United States. The feature lets users connect Apple Health and supported medical records so ChatGPT can interpret their information in context, track changes over time, and support more informed conversations. OpenAI said more than 300 million people ask ChatGPT health-related questions each week and that the feature was built with input from early testers and physicians.
