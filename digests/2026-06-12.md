## DiffusionGemma benchmarks 4x faster, 6x more error-prone

Atomic Chat published benchmarks pitting Google's new DiffusionGemma against its autoregressive Gemma 4 twin on a single H100 in FP8. The team said the diffusion model ran roughly 4x faster but produced about 6x more mistakes across three writing tasks, including a Steve Jobs biography, a Tetris history, and a BeOS retrospective.
