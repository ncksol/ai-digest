## Deletion Tuning matches fine-tuning by zeroing weights

A NeurIPS 2026 paper introduces Deletion Tuning, which zeroes fewer than 0.1% of a model's weights and matches the accuracy of full fine-tuning at scale. The authors say the resulting edits are thousands of times smaller and avoid catastrophic forgetting.

Geodesic has released NovaAtom-Lite, an all-atom structure prediction model for proteins, nucleic acids and bound molecules.
