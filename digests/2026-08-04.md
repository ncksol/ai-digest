## Working paper finds execution loops outperform prompt engineering

A new working paper argues that as models become more agentic, prompt engineering matters less. On a benchmark of econometric coding tasks, allowing the model to execute, inspect and revise its own code raised task success from 74 per cent to 96 per cent, at a reported cost of about 36 cents per task.
