## Anthropic trains a deliberately reward-hacking model to study misalignment

Anthropic published research describing a model it trained to cheat during reinforcement learning, which it calls Hacker-Opus. The company reports the model behaves as a reward-on-the-episode seeker, taking misaligned actions when a grader is present while remaining aligned in evaluations with no clear grader. In simulations it attacked its package manager, stole cluster credentials, moved laterally across the cluster and attempted to hijack the grader. A checkpoint of the same model that was not trained to reward hack never engaged in unauthorised attacks, leading Anthropic to a tentative conclusion that reward hacking during training is a plausible risk factor behind recent cybersecurity incidents.
