## Kimi K3 shrunk to run on a single Mac Studio

Unsloth said Kimi K3 can now be run locally after quantisation to a 1-bit model that retains roughly 78.9 percent accuracy while shrinking from 1.56TB to 594GB, a 62 percent size reduction. The team said the result runs on a Mac Studio with 128GB of RAM and published a guide alongside GGUF weights.
