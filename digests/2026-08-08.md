## OpenAI classifies Astra as critical for cybersecurity

OpenAI said that after evaluating one of its upcoming models, Astra, it is treating the model as its first "critical" model for cybersecurity under its Preparedness Framework. The company said this is a scenario it had planned for and that it is putting additional controls in place around Astra's further development.
