## Researchers outline five stages of recursive self-improvement

A 75-page paper from researchers at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, Tsinghua University, ByteDance and Shanghai AI Lab proposes five stages of recursive self-improvement. The sequence runs from executing human-designed improvements through choosing strategies and data to continuous adaptation and modifying the process used to create successor systems.
