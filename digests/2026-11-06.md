## Ryan Greenblatt says METR report implies around 2x speedup from AI R&D automation

METR researcher Ryan Greenblatt wrote that the public METR report indicates AI progress is currently sped up about 2x by recursive self-improvement, more than the 1.15x-1.3x he had expected for 2026. Using his prior public modelling, he estimates this corresponds to roughly a 4.5x acceleration of AI software progress, a level the AI 2027 scenario placed around March 2027.

## Memorilla compresses LLM memory into 16 embeddings

Researchers introduced Memorilla, which turns everything a frozen LLM needs to remember into 16 memory embeddings. The authors report up to 4,212 times fewer tokens and 27 times faster inference than full context, with higher accuracy than RAG and agentic memory on personalisation and long-document tasks.
