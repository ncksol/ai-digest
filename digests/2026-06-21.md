## Stanford method has chatbots interview themselves to write better reports

A post relaying work from Stanford professor Monica Lam argues that AI writes shallow reports because users ask a single question. The described method instead poses dozens of sub-questions in the manner of a journalist, and reports that the same chatbot then produces articles roughly 25% better organised than a top model answering directly.
