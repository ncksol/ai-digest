# 13 June 2026

## US issues export control directive against Claude Fable 5 and Mythos 5

Anthropic said the US government, citing national security authorities, has issued an export control directive suspending all access to Claude Fable 5 and Mythos 5 by any foreign national, whether inside or outside the United States, including Anthropic's own foreign-national employees. The company said it is complying while seeking clarification, and that the directive does not affect other Claude models.

## Levie calls directive a turning point for AI regulation

Box CEO Aaron Levie said the Fable 5 directive is a turning point for AI regulation because the US government is now treating specific frontier models as too powerful for certain uses, setting a precedent for further controls. He argued that the better policy lever is regulating the use of AI rather than the underlying models.
