## OpenAI expands Daybreak with the full GPT-5.5-Cyber model and a Patch the Planet program

OpenAI expanded its Daybreak security effort, releasing the full version of its GPT-5.5-Cyber model alongside a Codex Security plugin that finds, validates and fixes vulnerabilities inside Codex, and a new Cyber Partner Program. The company also launched Patch the Planet, an initiative to move open-source maintainers from security findings to merged fixes, working with Trail of Bits and HackerOne. Sam Altman said the model reaches state-of-the-art performance on the CyberGym benchmark and is meant to solve security problems rather than only find them.

## A large audit questions the reliability of LLM-as-a-Judge evaluation

A research summary described the largest LLM-as-a-Judge reliability audit to date, running 21 judges from nine providers across roughly 541,000 judgments on the MT-Bench, JudgeBench and RewardBench datasets. It found that validating a judge with exact-match agreement overstates its true skill, because exact-match comparison fails to capture how the judge performs.
