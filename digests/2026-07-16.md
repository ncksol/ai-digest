## OpenAI introduces GPT-Red, an automated red-teaming system

OpenAI unveiled GPT-Red, an internal automated red teamer built to find prompt-injection vulnerabilities in its models at scale before wider deployment. The system learns through adversarial self-play against defender models, and OpenAI said training against it made GPT-5.6 Sol its most robust model against prompt injections to date.
