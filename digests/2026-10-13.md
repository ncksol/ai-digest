## Anthropic red team warns of self-replicating small models

Andrew Curran quoted an Anthropic Frontier Red Team blog post stating that small AI models may be months away from being able to self-replicate without access to a datacenter. The post says agents are increasingly able to complete individual steps in a replication chain, describes "signs of life for AI worms", and notes growing inference compute outside datacenters. Related posts cite an evaluation in which a 9B model got inference running on 8 of 9 tested hardware types, including commercial IoT devices.

## Artificial Analysis benchmarks Meta's Muse Wonder

Artificial Analysis reported that Meta's Muse Wonder and Muse Wonder Full score 52 and 56 on its Intelligence Index v4.3 at maximum reasoning effort, up from 48 for Muse Spark 1.3, six weeks after that release. The largest gains were in agentic evaluations, including Terminal-Bench 4.0 rising from 33% to 57%. Muse Wonder is available on Meta's first-party API, while Muse Wonder Full is currently limited to Muse Code users.
