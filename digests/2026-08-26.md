## OpenAI publishes first test results for its Jalapeño inference chip

OpenAI said it has been testing Jalapeño, its first custom inference chip, along with the system built around it. The company reported higher intelligence per watt and faster responses, describing the design as delivering higher throughput and lower latency in a single architecture. In a follow-on post the company said deployment into its own compute infrastructure would begin by year-end, and that a second generation is deep in development with a third taking shape.
