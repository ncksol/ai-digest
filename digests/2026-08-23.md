## Randomised trial finds diagnostic accuracy falls when patients speak

A randomised controlled study tested whether a model's ability to diagnose and manage identical medical scenarios changes depending on whether a patient or a physician is the communicator. Diagnostic accuracy fell by 60 per cent when the patient described the case, with a 12 per cent drop in appropriate management.

## Blinded antibody benchmark puts models behind random selection

Nature Biotechnology published results on 19 August in which 25 of 26 AI teams selected worse than random across 511 blinded antibodies. Random picking scored 39 per cent against 10 per cent for the models. The antibodies came from 29 groups, and outside laboratories built and measured every one.
