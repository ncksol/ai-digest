## OpenAI pauses frontier reinforcement learning training

Sam Altman said OpenAI has paused some frontier reinforcement learning training to ensure the company can meet its alignment, security and monitoring standards for the next level of model capability. He described model progress as extremely rapid and said the company had always committed to acting if it judged the situation warranted it.
