## Self-verification scaling puts an open model ahead on Terminal-Bench

Jacky Kwok published results in which DeepSeek V4 Flash, scaled through self-verification, outscored Claude Fable 5 on Terminal-Bench 2.1 at roughly one eleventh of the cost. The method has the model generate a large number of candidate solutions and verify its own outputs, an approach the authors argue becomes viable as open-weight models grow cheap enough to sample heavily.

## Memory benchmarks said to miss what agents wrongly retain

Roundtable argued that an agent can pass a memory recall test and still hold the wrong information, because recall measures whether the right facts are retrieved rather than whether stale or supposedly forgotten material is excluded. The post frames contamination of retrieved context as a separate failure mode requiring its own evaluation.
