# 19 November 2026

## Google releases Gemini 4 Neon

Google has released Gemini 4 Neon, a model aimed at fast end-to-end task completion across software engineering, knowledge work and science workflows. It has a 1M-token context window and four adjustable thinking levels. Google says it improves multi-file debugging and refactoring and recorded its lowest fabrication rate yet in internal Google Antigravity testing. A demonstration showed a 12-hour Antigravity swarm reconstructing Alan Turing's 1936 study from archival photos.

## Codex used to find critical Java4Shell flaw

Security start-up Calif found a critical flaw, dubbed Java4Shell, in OpenJDK's Base64 decoder. Researcher Quang Luong used OpenAI's Codex to find it in about an hour and build a working exploit in about two more, for under $200 in compute. Rated 9.2 out of 10, it can be triggered by 64 'A' characters followed by '=' and was demonstrated against Keycloak. Oracle and OpenJDK have released fixes.
