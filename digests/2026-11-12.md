## Claude Fable 5.2 takes first place in Agent Arena

Arena reported that Claude Fable 5.2 (Max) ranks first in Agent Arena with a +30.4% net improvement across 4,700 real-world agentic sessions, about 1.6 times the second-placed Claude Opus 5.5.
