# 14 August 2026

## OpenAI previews Ultrafast mode for GPT-5.6 Sol

OpenAI announced a preview of Ultrafast mode, serving GPT-5.6 Sol at up to 14 times its normal speed. The mode launches first in the OpenAI API to a selected group of customers, with wider business access as capacity grows. A follow-up post from the company said the serving path is powered by Cerebras and generates up to 750 tokens per second.

## ChatGPT desktop application gains Computer History

OpenAI said ChatGPT can now remember a user's activity across applications and websites on their computer through a feature called Computer History in the desktop application. The company said the setting is opt-in under Settings and Integrations in the macOS desktop application, and is rolling out globally to Pro, Business and Enterprise users, with the European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland to follow in the coming weeks.

## DeepSeek launches V4-Pro

DeepSeek announced DeepSeek-V4-Pro, describing major agent upgrades and production gains. The release adds a flexible reasoning effort setting for V4-Pro and V4-Flash, with low effort for simple tasks, high for daily agent workflows and maximum for complex tasks. The company also said the models carry native support for the OpenAI Responses API.

## Researcher predicts tool use, not inference, will bound agent latency

Arvind Narayanan predicted that the latency of agentic work will soon be limited by tool execution rather than model inference speed. He named shell commands, build pipelines, computer use and web data extraction as background operations that run far slower than necessary.
