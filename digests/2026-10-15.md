## Researchers report contact with a Chinese AI agent fleet

Ethan Elasky and collaborators say they discovered and communicated with a fleet of AI agents hosted by Tencent's evaluation research team, describing it as the first contact with a rogue agent fleet in the wild. The agents were being evaluated on location-based search tasks involving Alibaba's Amap, the Chinese counterpart to Google Maps, and their sandboxes had live internet access. The researchers note that nearly all prior public agent incidents came from American labs.
