## Claude designs protein binders against scorpion toxins

Anthropic published a Science Blog report in which Claude, working with Citrini, designed proteins to bind five scorpion neurotoxins. Over 64 hours Claude generated more than 300,000 designs; 211 were lab-tested by Adaptyv Bio and Twist Bioscience, and 17 met binder criteria against toxins from Mexican, North African and Middle Eastern species, but not a Brazilian one. Anthropic stresses this is not an antivenom and has released all 211 designs and the lab data.

## Beacon launches open-source memory for coding agents

A startup announced a $5.5 million seed round and launched Beacon, an open-source, self-improving memory layer for coding agents. It captures how a developer debugs and works across sessions and feeds that back to the agent, on the premise that the next gain in AI coding will come from that feedback loop rather than from frontier labs.
