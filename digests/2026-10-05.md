## Karpathy says most of today's AI audience arrived within a year

Elvis Saravia questioned why a recent Andrej Karpathy post about agents drew 50,000 likes when, in his view, much of it described practices developers had used for over a year, while asking whether a universal interface for agent-to-agent communication exists. Karpathy replied that more than 99% of people now paying attention to AI were onboarded within the past year, which he said confuses longer-serving practitioners who entered the field before 2026 or before 2012.
