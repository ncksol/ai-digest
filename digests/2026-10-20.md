## Skyfall AI introduces SKY-001 engineering world model

Skyfall AI introduced SKY-001, which it describes as the first engineering world model, aimed at CAD and BIM design tasks. The company says that in its tests SKY-001 beat GPT-6 Astra and every other frontier model it tested, running 51 to 125 times faster, at 150 to 210 times lower cost, and with higher IoU scores. A follow-up thread argues that autoregressive LLMs are poorly suited to visual reasoning and long-horizon engineering work.

## Inception launches Mercury 3 diffusion LLM

Inception released Mercury 3, which it calls the most capable diffusion language model released so far. The company says it outperforms GPT-6 Luna and Claude Haiku 5.5 on a range of benchmarks while generating more than 1,000 tokens per second. Mercury 3 is more than 40 times the size of Mercury 1 and scores three times higher on standard benchmarks, according to the company.
