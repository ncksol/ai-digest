## Coinbase CEO describes running lead agents and a chief-of-staff agent

Brian Armstrong said he now runs one always-on lead agent per area of focus, overseen by a chief-of-staff agent. Each lead manages sub-agents that write code, test it, chase reviews and coordinate with colleagues. Decisions reach him in a single prioritised queue with screenshots and video demos, which he clears by voice and keyboard shortcuts. He said every change still goes through the same human review and security checks.
