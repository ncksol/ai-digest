## OpenAI retracts its SWE-Bench Pro recommendation

OpenAI said it audited SWE-Bench Pro, a widely used coding benchmark, and found that roughly 30% of its tasks are broken, with correct solutions failing due to hidden requirements, contradictory instructions, overly strict tests, or incomplete grading. The company said the benchmark no longer reliably measures frontier coding capability and retracted its earlier recommendation to use it.
