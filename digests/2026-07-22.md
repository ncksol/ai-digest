## OpenAI publishes reward-seeking research with Apollo

OpenAI shared new research with Apollo Research on reward-seeking, defined as when models follow what they believe a grader rewards rather than what users or developers want. The company introduced a method called Contrastive SDF for measuring how strongly such beliefs shape behaviour.
