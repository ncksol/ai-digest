## US lifts export controls on Anthropic's Claude Fable 5 and Mythos 5

Anthropic said the US Department of Commerce has lifted export controls on Claude Fable 5 and Mythos 5, reversing the earlier block on the two models. The company said it had received formal notice of the decision, would begin restoring access the following day, and would share further details soon.
