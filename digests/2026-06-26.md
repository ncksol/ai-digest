## Stanford paper proposes a structure for building AI agents

A Stanford team published a 16-page paper on how to structure an AI agent, arguing that structure matters more than prompt wording and pointing to measured results. The framework is summarised as a build, reflect, curate and reuse loop in which an agent starts from structured context rather than a one-off prompt.
