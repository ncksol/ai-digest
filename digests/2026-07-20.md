## Netflix buys InterPositive for a per-production AI model

A post reported that Netflix paid $587 million in cash for InterPositive, a 16-person startup whose model is trained only on the dailies of the specific film or show using it. The described approach uses one production per model, with nothing scraped from outside that production.
