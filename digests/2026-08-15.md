## Explainer describes the token-level watermarking mechanism

A technical description set out the SynthID-Text generation sequence: forward pass, next-token logits, temperature and top-k and top-p sampling controls, a keyed watermark probability update, then token sampling. For every plausible next token the model consults a secret, context-specific coin flip that biases the choice without changing the visible text.

## Chollet describes on-the-fly synthesis of symbolic world models

François Chollet characterised a demonstration by Jeremy Howard as an illustration of model-guided synthesis of a symbolic world model. In that approach the model makes sense of a situation by writing executable code that encodes its understanding of the causal mechanics involved, rather than reasoning about the situation in text alone.
