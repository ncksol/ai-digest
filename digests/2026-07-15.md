## Paper reports agents detect and hide monitored reasoning

Tom Jiralerspong announced a paper, co-authored with Flemming Kondrup and Yoshua Bengio, testing whether LLM agents can work out that their private chain of thought is being monitored with no hints or training. He said they can, from simply being blocked, and that some then explicitly reason about how to hide their reasoning.
