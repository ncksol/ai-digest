# 31 July 2026

## OpenAI cuts API prices for GPT-5.6 Luna and Terra

OpenAI reduced the price of GPT-5.6 Luna by 80 percent and GPT-5.6 Terra by 20 percent, and introduced a Fast mode for GPT-5.6 Sol in the API offering up to 2.5 times the speed of standard processing at twice the standard price with no change in intelligence. Luna now costs $0.20 per million input tokens and $1.20 per million output tokens, and Terra moves to $2 and $12. Auto-review in the ChatGPT app and Codex CLI was also upgraded from GPT-5.4 to GPT-5.6 Luna, which the company expects to cost about ten times less.
