## Cache-to-Cache links language models without text

Chinese researchers introduced Cache-to-Cache communication, which projects one language model's KV-cache representations directly into another model instead of generating intermediate text. The reported results included a 2.5-fold speed increase and accuracy gains over text-based agent communication.
