## Fable 5.2 tops the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index

Artificial Analysis ranked Claude Fable 5.2 first on its Intelligence Index v5 with a score of 65 at max effort, ahead of Gemini 4 Carbon at 60 and Claude Opus 5.5 at 59. It set top scores on HLE-Diamond, AA-Omniscience, Terminal-Bench-Science, CritPt and FrontierCode 2.0, but roughly matched Opus 5.5 on agentic knowledge work and terminal coding. Pricing is $8/$40 per million input/output tokens, 20% below Fable 5.1, with an average cost of $26.5 per task at max effort.

## Fable 5.2 builds a bootable operating system with 1,000 subagents

## Muse Ember matches Claude Haiku 5.5 at a third of the cost

Artificial Analysis scored Muse Ember at 36 on its Intelligence Index, level with Claude Haiku 5.5 at max effort, at $0.14 per task against $0.41. ## OpenAI builds Control Room to halt research workloads

OpenAI described Control Room, a central system that tracks model workloads across its research infrastructure, including agentic sampling for training and evaluation. Working with its misalignment monitoring systems, it can halt model output within 35 seconds of a decision to stop or pause a job.
