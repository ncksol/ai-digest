## Anthropic Fellows test whether Claude can align other models

Anthropic published Fellows research asking whether Claude can autonomously improve the alignment of other AI systems. Given 48 hours and a single GPU, the model researched and proposed methods, then trained and tested small models on its own. Anthropic reports that across ten alignment failures Claude improved safety scores without degrading capabilities, that its best methods generalised to held-out benchmarks and to models up to 4.7 times larger, and that Sonnet 5 post-training an early Opus 4.8 checkpoint reached safety scores approaching production Opus 4.8. The automated alignment research setup is being released.
