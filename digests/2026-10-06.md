## Reflection AI introduces the Beam open model

Reflection AI introduced Beam, an agentic open model with 501 billion total parameters and 23 billion active parameters. The company said the model was trained end-to-end from scratch, targets reasoning efficiency, and advances Western open models on coding and agentic tasks. Full weights are scheduled for release this month.

## COLM paper tests whether LLMs know why they decide

A new COLM paper, Identifying Introspection From the Inside, examines whether language models reporting on their decisions know what drives them or are guessing. The authors found that faithful models make and report decisions using the same layers, while unfaithful models do not.
