## Notion disables Anthropic models after Opus degradation

Notion's status account announced that Anthropic's Opus 4.7 and 4.8 models were experiencing degraded performance and a higher failure rate inside Notion AI. All Anthropic models were temporarily disabled in the model picker, with requests rerouted to other providers while Anthropic investigated.

## Paper argues research agents win by persistence, not brilliance

A joint paper from Stanford, MIT, NVIDIA, Google and other labs, summarised by Rohan Paul, argues today's strongest research agents struggle with long-horizon work and that performance gains come less from raw model capability than from refusing to stop testing and iterating.
